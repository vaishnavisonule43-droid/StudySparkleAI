📌 Introduction to Digital Marketing

Learn the basic concepts of Digital Marketing.

What is Digital Marketing?

Digital Marketing is the process of promoting products, services or brands using digital platforms such as websites, search engines, social media and email.

Definition: Digital Marketing means promoting products or services through digital channels and the internet.

Features of Digital Marketing

  • It uses digital platforms and the internet.
  • It helps businesses reach a large audience.
  • It provides measurable results.
  • It allows direct communication with customers.
  • It can be more cost-effective than traditional marketing.

Digital Marketing Channels

1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

SEO helps a website appear higher in search engine results.

2. Social Media Marketing

It promotes products and services through platforms such as Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

3. Email Marketing

It uses emails to communicate with customers and promote products or services.

4. Content Marketing

It involves creating useful content such as blogs, videos and posts to attract customers.

Advantages of Digital Marketing

  • Reaches a large number of customers.
  • Available 24/7.
  • Easy to measure results.
  • Helps target specific customers.
  • Saves time and marketing cost.

Example of Digital Marketing

For example, a clothing shop can promote its new clothes on Instagram and Facebook. Customers can see the products, visit the website and place an order online.

Simple Example: Instagram advertisement → Customer sees product → Customer visits website → Customer purchases product.
