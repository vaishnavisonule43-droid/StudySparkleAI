Cyber Safety

Learn how to stay safe while using the internet.

📘 Introduction

Cyber Safety means using computers, mobile phones, websites and the internet safely and responsibly.

It helps protect our personal information, online accounts, devices and money from online threats.

📖 1. Definition of Cyber Safety

Cyber Safety is the practice of protecting ourselves and our information from dangers while using digital devices and the internet.

Example: Using a strong password and not sharing an OTP with anyone.

🎯 2. Objectives of Cyber Safety

  • To protect personal information.
  • To prevent online fraud.
  • To keep online accounts secure.
  • To protect devices from malware.
  • To avoid cyberbullying and online harassment.
  • To develop safe internet habits.

⚠️ 3. Common Cyber Safety Threats

1. Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites try to steal passwords and banking information.

2. Malware

Harmful software can damage a device or steal information.

3. Identity Theft

Someone uses another person's personal information without permission.

4. Cyberbullying

Using online platforms to threaten, insult or repeatedly harass someone.

5. Online Financial Fraud

Scammers trick people into sending money or revealing banking details.

6. Password Theft

Attackers try to obtain passwords to access personal accounts.

🔐 4. Important Cyber Safety Rules

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for different accounts. Avoid using easily guessed information.

2. Never Share OTPs

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.

3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use an additional verification step to make your online accounts more secure.

4. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links or download unexpected attachments.

5. Update Your Devices

Install operating system and application updates to fix security problems.

6. Use Secure Websites

Check the website address carefully before entering personal or payment information.

7. Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Sensitive Work

Avoid accessing banking services over untrusted public Wi-Fi. Use a trusted network whenever possible.

8. Take Care on Social Media

Do not publicly share your address, passwords, bank details or other sensitive information.

📱 5. Mobile and Computer Safety

  • Use a screen lock on your mobile phone.
  • Install applications from trusted sources.
  • Review app permissions before allowing access.
  • Use reputable security software when appropriate.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Do not use unknown USB drives on your computer.
  • Log out from accounts on shared computers.

🌐 6. Social Media Safety

  • Keep your account privacy settings updated.
  • Accept friend requests carefully.
  • Verify information before sharing it.
  • Never share private photos with strangers.
  • Block and report abusive accounts.
  • Think before posting anything online.

🚨 7. What to Do If You Face Cybercrime

  1. Stop communicating with the suspected scammer.
  2. Change affected passwords from a trusted device.
  3. Contact your bank immediately if money is involved.
  4. Save relevant messages, screenshots and transaction details.
  5. Report the incident through official channels.

India Cybercrime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in

Financial cyber fraud helpline: 1930. Report quickly if money has been lost.

⭐ 8. Advantages of Cyber Safety

  • Protects personal information.
  • Reduces the risk of online fraud.
  • Helps keep devices and accounts secure.
  • Promotes safe online communication.
  • Builds confidence while using digital services.

⚡ 9. Challenges of Cyber Safety

  • New cyber threats appear regularly.
  • Users may not recognize fake messages.
  • Weak passwords can expose accounts.
  • Online scams can be difficult to identify.
  • Users need to keep learning safe practices.

📝 10. Conclusion

Cyber Safety is essential for everyone who uses computers and the internet. By using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links and protecting personal information, we can reduce online risks and use technology more safely.
