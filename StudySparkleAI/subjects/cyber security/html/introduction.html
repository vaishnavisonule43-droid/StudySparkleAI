Introduction to Cyber Security

Learn the basics of protecting computers, networks and data.

📘 Introduction

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, software and data from unauthorized access, cyber attacks and damage.

It helps keep our personal information, passwords, bank details and important files safe from hackers.

📖 1. Definition of Cyber Security

Cyber Security is the protection of computers, networks and digital information from cyber threats and attacks.

Example: Using a strong password and two-factor authentication to protect an online account.

🛡️ 2. Need for Cyber Security

🔒 Data Protection

It protects personal and confidential information.

💳 Online Safety

It helps protect online banking and digital payments.

🦠 Virus Protection

It helps protect devices from viruses and malware.

👤 Privacy

It helps prevent unauthorized access to personal data.

🎯 3. Objectives of Cyber Security

1. Confidentiality

It means only authorized people can access the data.

Example: Keeping your password secret.

2. Integrity

It means keeping data correct and preventing unauthorized changes.

Example: Preventing someone from changing your bank balance illegally.

3. Availability

It means information and services should be available to authorized users when needed.

Example: Accessing your college website when you need your results.

🧩 4. Types of Cyber Security

Network Security

Protects computer networks from attacks.

Application Security

Protects software and applications from threats.

Information Security

Protects digital and other important information.

Cloud Security

Protects data and services stored in the cloud.

Mobile Security

Protects smartphones and mobile applications.

Operational Security

Protects data through safe handling and access rules.

⚠️ 5. Common Cyber Threats

1. Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or steal data.

2. Phishing

Fake messages or websites used to steal personal information.

3. Hacking

Unauthorized access to computer systems or accounts.

4. Password Attacks

Attempts to discover or steal someone's password.

5. Ransomware

Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

💻 6. Examples of Cyber Security

  • Using strong and unique passwords.
  • Installing antivirus software.
  • Using two-factor authentication.
  • Keeping software and operating systems updated.
  • Using secure websites for online payments.
  • Avoiding suspicious links and attachments.

⭐ 7. Importance of Cyber Security

  • Protects personal and business data.
  • Reduces the risk of cyber attacks.
  • Protects online transactions.
  • Maintains privacy and trust.
  • Helps organizations continue their work safely.
  • Prevents unauthorized access to systems.

✅ 8. Basic Cyber Safety Rules

✔️ Use strong and unique passwords.

✔️ Never share OTPs or passwords.

✔️ Do not click unknown links.

✔️ Enable two-factor authentication.

✔️ Update apps and software regularly.

✔️ Back up important files.

✔️ Download files only from trusted sources.

📝 9. Conclusion

Cyber Security is important in today's digital world. It protects our devices, networks and information from cyber threats. Following safe online practices helps us use the internet securely.
