🔐 Cyber Security

Learn how computers, networks, websites and digital information are protected from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

📖 Introduction

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from cyber threats. It helps prevent unauthorized access, data theft, malware attacks and other online security problems. It is important for keeping personal and organizational information safe.

  • Phishing and Online Fraud
  • Network Security
  • Information Security
  • Encryption and Decryption
  • Authentication and Authorization
  • Firewalls and Antivirus Software
  • Cyber Laws and Ethics

⭐ Why Is Cyber Security Important?

  • Protects personal and confidential information.
  • Prevents unauthorized access to computer systems.
  • Helps protect computers from malware and viruses.
  • Reduces the risk of online fraud and data theft.
  • Improves the security of networks and websites.
  • Helps maintain privacy and data integrity.

💡 Where Is Cyber Security Used?

  • Banking and Online Payments
  • Government Organizations
  • Hospitals and Healthcare Systems
  • Educational Institutions
  • Business Websites and Applications
  • Social Media Platforms
  • E-Commerce Websites
  • Computer Networks and Cloud Systems
