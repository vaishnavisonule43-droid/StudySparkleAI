⚡ Agile Model

Learn the basic concepts of Agile Software Development Model.

📌 What is Agile Model?

Agile Model is a software development approach in which software is developed step by step in small parts. It focuses on continuous improvement, customer feedback and quick delivery.

Definition: Agile Model is an iterative and flexible software development model where software is developed in small increments and continuously improved.

⭐ Features of Agile Model

  • Iterative Development: Software is developed in small cycles.
  • Customer Involvement: Customer feedback is taken regularly.
  • Flexible: Changes in requirements can be accepted easily.
  • Fast Delivery: Working software is delivered quickly.
  • Continuous Testing: Testing is performed regularly during development.

🔄 Agile Process

  1. Collect and understand requirements.
  2. Plan the work for a small iteration.
  3. Design and develop the software.
  4. Test the developed software.
  5. Show the working product to the customer.
  6. Take feedback and improve the software.
  7. Repeat the process for the next iteration.

💡 Advantages of Agile Model

  • Changes can be easily accepted.
  • Customer satisfaction is improved.
  • Working software is delivered quickly.
  • Errors can be identified early.
  • Continuous improvement is possible.

⚠️ Disadvantages of Agile Model

  • Requires regular customer involvement.
  • Requirements may change frequently.
  • Less suitable when requirements are fixed and stable.
  • Continuous communication is required.
  • Project management can become difficult for large projects.

📊 Agile vs Waterfall

Agile Waterfall
Development is done in small iterations. Development follows fixed sequential phases.
Changes can be accepted easily. Changes are difficult after a phase is completed.
Customer feedback is taken regularly. Customer involvement is mainly during requirements.
Working software is delivered frequently. Final product is generally delivered at the end.

🌟 Example of Agile Model

Example: Suppose a college wants to develop a college management system. First, the team develops the student registration module. After testing and customer feedback, the team develops the attendance module, then the examination module and finally other modules. In this way, the system is developed step by step.
