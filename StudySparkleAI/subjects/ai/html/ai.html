📖 Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks such as learning, problem-solving, decision-making and understanding language. AI is used in chatbots, voice assistants, healthcare, education and many other fields. Examples include ChatGPT, Google Assistant and AI-powered recommendation systems.

⭐ Why Is Artificial Intelligence Important?

AI helps computers analyse information, automate repetitive tasks and support decision-making. It can save time, improve productivity and help solve problems in different industries. However, AI also requires responsible use, data protection and human supervision.
