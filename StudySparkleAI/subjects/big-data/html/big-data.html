📊 Big Data

Explore large datasets and learn how data helps in decision-making.

📖 Introduction

Big Data refers to very large and complex amounts of data that traditional tools cannot easily process. It helps organizations analyze information, discover patterns and make better decisions.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to Big Data
  • Characteristics of Big Data
  • Types and Sources of Data
  • Big Data Technologies
  • Hadoop and HDFS
  • MapReduce
  • Big Data Analytics
  • Data Security and Privacy

⭐ Why Is Big Data Important?

Big Data helps organizations understand customer behavior, identify trends, improve services and make informed decisions. It is useful in business, healthcare, education and many other fields.

💡 Where Is Big Data Used?

  • Healthcare and Medical Research
  • Banking and Finance
  • Online Shopping
  • Social Media Platforms
  • Education
  • Weather Forecasting
  • Business Analytics
  • Transportation
